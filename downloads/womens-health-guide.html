A gentle guide to women's health

A short, general-purpose guide for preparing questions and keeping track of changes you notice.

Notice and record

  • Write down the date and what you noticed.
  • For menstrual changes, note the first day of bleeding and how long bleeding lasts.
  • Record other details that matter to you, such as pain, sleep, mood or how symptoms affect daily activities.

When to ask for care

Consider talking with a qualified healthcare professional about new, persistent, changing or worrying symptoms. Seek urgent medical care for sudden severe pain, fainting, or very heavy bleeding—especially if you feel weak, dizzy or unwell.

This guide is for education, not diagnosis or treatment. Cycle-based predictions cannot reliably prevent pregnancy.
